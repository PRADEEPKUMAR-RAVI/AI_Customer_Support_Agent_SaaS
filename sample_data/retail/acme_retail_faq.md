# Acme Retail — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. For full policies (returns, shipping, warranty,
payments), see the Acme Retail Help Center.

---

## Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship to most countries. International orders use **DHL Express** and typically
arrive in **7–12 business days**. Customs duties and import taxes are calculated at
checkout and paid upfront, so there are no surprise fees on delivery. A few restricted
categories (aerosols, large lithium batteries) can only ship domestically.

## What if my item arrives damaged?

If your order arrives damaged or defective, report it within **48 hours of delivery**.
We'll send a prepaid return label and ship a replacement at no cost, or issue a full
refund — your choice. Keep the original packaging until the claim is resolved.

## Do you offer gift wrapping or gift receipts?

Yes. At checkout, select **Add gift wrap** ($4.99 per item) and optionally include a
gift message. A gift receipt hides the price and lets the recipient return the item for
store credit without seeing the original payment method.

## Can I use more than one discount code?

No — only **one promotional code** can be applied per order, and it must be entered
before payment. Store credit and gift cards, however, can be combined with a promo code.

## Do you price match?

Yes. If you find the same item at a lower advertised price from a major retailer within
**14 days** of your purchase, contact support with a link and we'll refund the
difference to your original payment method.

## How does the Acme Rewards loyalty program work?

Acme Rewards members earn **1 point per $1 spent**, and **100 points = $5** in store
credit. Points post after an order is delivered and expire 12 months after they are
earned. Membership is free — sign up under **Account Settings → Rewards**.

## Can I place a bulk or business order?

For orders of **25+ units** of a single item, our business team offers volume pricing
and invoicing. Email **business@acme.test** with the item and quantity for a quote.

## Is there a subscription or auto-reorder option?

Yes — eligible consumable items (filters, refills) offer **Subscribe & Save**: pick a
delivery interval and save 5%. You can pause, skip, or cancel a subscription anytime
from **Account Settings → Subscriptions** with no fee.

## What are your customer support hours?

The chat assistant is available **24×7**. Human agents reply to email within one
business day, and the phone line (1-800-555-ACME) is open Monday–Friday, 8 AM–8 PM.
