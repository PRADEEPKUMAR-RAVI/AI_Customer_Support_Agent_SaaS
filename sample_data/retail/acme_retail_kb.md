# Acme Retail — Customer Help Center

Welcome to the Acme Retail knowledge base. This document covers our policies for
returns, shipping, warranties, order tracking, payments, and account management.
It is the source of truth the support assistant uses to answer customer questions.

---

## Returns & Refunds

We want you to love what you ordered. If you don't, most items can be returned
within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to the original payment method.

### How to start a return

1. Sign in to your Acme account and open **Order History**.
2. Select the order and click **Return or Replace Items**.
3. Choose a reason and print the prepaid return label.
4. Drop the package at any partner carrier location within 7 days.
5. Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund is issued within
   **5–7 business days**.

### What can be returned

- Unused items in original packaging: full refund.
- Opened items in resellable condition: refund minus a 10% restocking fee.
- Final-sale, personalized, and perishable items: **not returnable**.
- Gift cards and digital downloads: **not returnable**.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. If the card is no longer
active, we issue store credit instead.

## Shipping & Delivery

We ship to all serviceable addresses in the country. Delivery estimates are shown
at checkout and depend on the option you choose.

| Shipping option | Cost           | Estimated delivery      |
|-----------------|----------------|-------------------------|
| Standard        | Free over $35  | 3–5 business days       |
| Expedited       | $9.99          | 2 business days         |
| Next-Day        | $19.99         | 1 business day          |
| Store Pickup    | Free           | Same day if in stock    |

Orders placed before 2:00 PM local time ship the same business day. We do not
ship on weekends or public holidays. A tracking reference is emailed as soon as
your order leaves the warehouse.

## Order Status & Tracking

Every order moves through the following statuses:

- **placed** — we received your order and payment is authorized.
- **processing** — the item is being picked and packed.
- **shipped** — the order has left our warehouse; a tracking reference is available.
- **delivered** — the carrier confirmed delivery to your address.
- **cancelled** — the order was cancelled and any charge was reversed.

To check your order, open **Order History** or ask the support assistant with your
**order number** and the **email** on the order. For privacy, we verify your email
before sharing order details.

If your tracking shows **delivered** but you have not received the package, please
contact us within 48 hours so we can open an investigation with the carrier.

## Warranty

Most Acme electronics and appliances include a **12-month limited warranty**
covering manufacturing defects from the delivery date.

- **active** — the item is within its coverage window and eligible for repair or replacement.
- **expired** — the coverage window has passed; paid repair may still be available.
- **void** — coverage was cancelled due to physical or liquid damage, unauthorized
  repair, or a missing serial number.

To make a warranty claim you need the product **serial number** (found on the box
or the device) or the original **order number**. The warranty does not cover
accidental damage, normal wear, or consumable parts such as batteries and filters.

## Payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Acme Store Credit. Payment is
authorized when you place the order and captured when the order ships. If an
authorization fails, the order is held for 24 hours before it is cancelled.

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address. Promotional
discount codes cannot be combined and must be applied before payment.

## Account & Privacy

You can update your email, shipping addresses, and payment methods under
**Account Settings**. We never share your personal details with third parties
except the carrier delivering your order. To close your account or request
deletion of your data, contact support and we will process it within 30 days.
